- A large copy no longer fails part way. A 13 GB copy that died at 84% now finishes intact.
- macOS no longer says the drive has stopped responding during a copy.
- Copying to a slow drive is faster: the worst pause fell from 90 seconds to 16.
- A drive Windows left dirty is repaired and opened for writing, rather than read-only.
- A drive Windows left hibernated is refused rather than written to.
- XFS volumes open, on their own and inside a LUKS container.
- The notice about early development is shown once, instead of at every launch.
- Quitting with a drive open asks once. It could ask twice, on two screens.
