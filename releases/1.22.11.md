- Files reach the drive when the app says they have, even if the Mac loses power a moment later.
- Copying to a drive is about twice as fast.
- A drive macOS has already mounted now opens straight away, instead of asking you to eject it first.
- Drives are named by what is on them: an exFAT stick is no longer called "BitLocker/NTFS".
- A drive whose partition table is out of date now appears in the list, and opens.
