- A drive is opened with a key you already saved, even when the drive no longer
  matches the name that key was saved under. Sticks that Windows prepares carry
  no name of their own, and a key saved while a drive was open is filed under
  what the drive called itself then — so the app could be holding your key and
  ask you for it anyway. It now tries what it has before asking, and a key that
  turns out to belong to another drive is passed over without a word.
- Whatever opens a drive is then kept under that drive's own name, so it is
  found straight away next time.
