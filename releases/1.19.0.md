- A drive that failed because the machinery slipped -- a broken pipe, an image
  the last machine had not finished letting go of -- is tried again rather than
  reported as a drive that cannot be opened.
- An attempt that will never finish is ended after eight minutes, rather than
  leaving something turning for as long as somebody is willing to watch it.
- A drive is shown as open only where something is mounted. A mount that failed
  used to leave an empty folder behind, and the app presented it as the drive.
- A container holding a volume group opens all of its volumes, and asks for
  nothing to do it.
- A drive that would have opened read-only because the machinery slipped opens
  for writing.
- Mounts and machines left behind by an earlier run are cleared before a drive
  is opened, so opening the next one is not refused as though the engine were
  busy.
- An update offered while a drive is open waits until the drive is ejected.
- A version that will not start puts the previous one back, and the copy it
  needs is kept until the update it was made for has been installed.
- "The drive could not be opened" is said in the reader's own language.
