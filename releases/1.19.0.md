- A drive that failed for a passing reason -- a broken pipe, an image the last
  machine had not let go of -- is tried again, rather than reported as a drive
  that will not open or quietly opened read-only.
- An attempt that cannot finish is ended after eight minutes instead of waiting
  for ever.
- A drive is shown as open only where something is really mounted. A failed
  mount used to leave an empty folder behind, and the app presented that as the
  drive.
- A container holding a volume group opens all of its volumes, without asking
  for a password.
- Leftovers from an earlier run are cleared before a drive opens, so the next
  one is not refused as busy.
- An update offered while a drive is open waits until the drive is ejected.
- A version that will not start puts the previous one back.
- "The drive could not be opened" is said in the reader's own language.
