- Copying a lot of files to a drive no longer fails part way through.
- macOS no longer says the drive has stopped responding while you are copying.
- Copying to a slow drive is faster.
- A drive Windows left in a bad state is repaired, so you can write to it again.
- A drive left hibernated by Windows is not written to, so Windows still resumes.
- XFS drives open, on their own and inside a LUKS container.
- The notice about early development appears once, not every time you open the app.
- Installing an update no longer asks whether to keep your drives open.
