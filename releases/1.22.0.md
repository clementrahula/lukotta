- Copying a lot of files to a drive no longer fails part way through, and the
  folder you are copying into keeps answering while it happens. Finder used to
  sit for several seconds before it would show you what was in it.
- macOS no longer says the drive has stopped responding while you are copying.
- Copying to a slow drive is faster.
- Files no longer go missing from a copy without anything being said. A copy
  that could not write a file now fails honestly instead of finishing quietly
  and leaving it out.
- A drive Windows left in a bad state is repaired so you can write to it, rather
  than being handed back to you read-only with an explanation.
- A drive left hibernated by Windows is not written to, so Windows still resumes.
- Drives that were being written to while the app refused them are no longer
  written to at all. The app checked whether it should open a drive after it had
  already touched it.
- XFS drives open, on their own and inside a LUKS container, and so does every
  other Linux filesystem the app lists.
- Drives that are part of a RAID array are read as the array they belong to
  instead of being mistaken for something else.
- Every disk image format the app says it can open has been opened, written to
  and read back.
- A drive you saved a password for opens without asking for it again, even when
  the drive no longer matches the name that password was saved under. Sticks
  Windows prepares carry no name of their own, and a locked drive tells macOS
  even less, so the app could be holding your password and ask you for it
  anyway. It now tries what it has before asking anyone.
- Saving the password is offered by default and stays how you set it. It used to
  arrive switched off, so a password you meant to keep was thrown away instead.
- A drive opens the first time you use the app, instead of saying it could not
  be opened.
- The app puts back anything missing from its own supporting files rather than
  refusing to open a drive, and two copies of the app installed side by side no
  longer disturb each other.
- Waiting for a drive to open no longer gives up early and leaves you looking at
  a progress bar that has stopped.
- The notice about early development appears once, not every time you open the
  app.
- Quitting with a drive open asks you once, and installing an update does not
  ask at all.
