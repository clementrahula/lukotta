- Lukotta can sit in the menu bar again, and this time it is drawn. The item
  offers to eject each open drive, and it stays there when the app is quit with
  a drive still open, so a drive is never left with nothing to eject it by.
- A drive that stopped responding, or a file served after a relaunch, keeps its
  own name in the list rather than reappearing as something else. A drive that
  is mounted is never taken out of the list by a scan that missed it.
- Dropping a row between two rows puts it between them.
- A saved passphrase that the Keychain already holds is written over rather
  than refused, so a key saved by an earlier version keeps working. Where the
  Keychain does refuse, the reason is written down instead of being lost.
- An update that installs a version this Mac cannot start puts the previous one
  back on its own, without waiting for three failed launches to notice.
- The background helper names the same engine directory as the app, and a
  helper too old to be asked to stand down no longer holds an update up.
