- A drive that goes quiet is no longer given up on. macOS stops waiting after
  forty-five seconds and takes the mount away, and the machine serving the drive
  shuts down with it, so a copy that met a slow moment ended there and could not
  be resumed. Five minutes now, which a drive can be slow in and still come back.
- A drive is never closed because a question about it went unanswered. Asking
  the mounting engine what it is serving can fail — on a busy Mac, it does — and
  the answer that came back was indistinguishable from "nothing is mounted",
  which read as a drive nobody was using and closed it mid-copy.
- A drive that has stopped answering is given a minute to start again before it
  is treated as gone, and is left alone entirely while the machine serving it is
  still running. Five seconds of silence used to be enough to close it.
- Files left behind by a drive that disappeared mid-copy are reported rather
  than quietly removed. They are on the startup disk, not on the drive, and the
  copy that wrote them was told it had succeeded.
- The background helper is replaced when it is not the one this version carries,
  without asking for anything, and nothing asks for an administrator password
  while the app is starting.
