- A drive you asked to be remembered is remembered. Keeping the key was
  offered but switched off unless you noticed the tick box, and a drive the
  app had nothing saved for switched it off again — so unlocking that drive
  cleared the key instead of keeping it, and the same stick asked every time.
  It now offers to keep the key by default, and leaves the switch alone once
  you have set it.
- A drive that comes back is recognised as the one it was. Sticks that Windows
  prepares carry no name of their own, and a locked drive tells macOS even
  less, so the app had been recognising them by where they were plugged in.
  They are now recognised by what is written on them, and a key saved earlier
  is found again and kept under the new name.
- Unlocking from the command line and unlocking from the window now look for a
  saved key in exactly the same way. They had drifted apart, and one of them
  could find a key on a drive where the other asked.
